🤔 What is AgentDisplay?

AgentDisplay is a smart desktop companion that brings your coding activities to life right in front of you. Using a small ESP32-S3 screen, it shows real-time status updates from your coding tools, lets you interact by voice, and throws in some fun offline reactions.

✨ Cool Things You Can Do

📡 Live Coding Status

AgentDisplay connects to your computer and shows what is happening in real time. See when your code is running, building, or when something needs your attention. It connects via WebSocket so everything updates instantly.

🎤 Talk To Your Agent

Use the built-in microphone on your ESP32-S3 to talk. The display listens, understands, and responds using voice (ASR + LLM + TTS). Ask questions, give commands, or just chat with your desktop buddy.

😊 Fun Offline Emoji

Even when you are not actively coding, your display shows cute emoji and reactions on the screen. It brings life to your desk without needing an internet connection.

📊 Simple Dashboard

A user-friendly FastAPI dashboard lets you see everything at a glance. Check device status, tweak settings, and see past activity from your browser.

📱 Easy BLE Setup

Set up your display in minutes using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). Pair it with your phone or computer, and you are ready to go. No complicated wiring or coding required.

  • 📷 An ESP32-S3 board with a screen (like the ESP32-S3-USB-OTG or similar)
  • 🔌 A USB cable to connect the board to your computer
  • 🌐 A stable Wi-Fi or Ethernet connection for WebSocket updates and voice features
  • 🎤 Optional: A microphone to use voice commands (most ESP32-S3 boards have one built-in)

🔧 Behind the Scenes (For the Curious)

AgentDisplay is built using ESP-IDF and LVGL for a smooth, beautiful display on your ESP32-S3. It uses WebSocket to push live coding status from your desktop, and a FastAPI backend handles the dashboard. Voice features run on-device with PDM microphone support, incorporating ASR, LLM, and TTS engines. BLE makes setup easy, and the whole system is designed to be lightweight and fast.
